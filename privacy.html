Privacy Policy

CalenderShare reads only the developer's own Google account data (Gmail, read-only) with the developer's consent. The data is stored only on the developer's own computer and is never sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties.

CalenderShare は開発者本人の同意のもとで、本人の Google アカウントのデータ（Gmail・読み取り専用）のみを読み取ります。データは本人のPCにのみ保存し、第三者への販売・共有・提供は行いません。

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
